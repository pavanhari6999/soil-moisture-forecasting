# Data provenance and copyright policy

## Source dataset

**AgriDataValue – Environmental Data Useful for Smart Irrigation**

Zenodo DOI: https://doi.org/10.18959383

The research source is the publicly accessible AgriDataValue dataset deposited at Zenodo:

https://zenodo.org/records/18959383

Four station-year datasets are covered:

| Dataset | Year | Crop |
|---|---:|---|
| Station 34 | 2024 | Sorghum (Jumbo Star) |
| Station 35 | 2024 | Corn (Forturio) |
| Station 34 | 2025 | Corn |
| Station 35 | 2025 | Corn |

Raw observations: **22,111**  
After documented quality control: **22,050**

## Redistribution policy

The original AgriDataValue files are **not included in this repository**. They are third-party source material and the current Zenodo record does not state an explicit redistribution license. Download the source files directly from Zenodo and follow the terms supplied by the data provider.

Do not commit the original Excel/CSV files, even if downloaded locally.

## External literature

Published papers used to establish the research context are cited in `paper/references.bib`. Publisher PDFs and copied article text are not stored in this repository.

## Generated materials

Only results and figures generated by the present analysis may be stored in the repository. A figure/table copied from another publication must not be added.
